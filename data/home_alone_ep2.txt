# TITLE: Home Alone (1990) — Part 2
# KIND: film script
# VOCAB: kevin, kate, mccallister_family, mccallister_phone, mccallister_house, alarm_clocks, house_power, wet_bandits, murphy_house

## SCENE 9
INT. MCCALLISTER HOUSE, KITCHEN - EVENING. Kevin, home alone, picks up the
kitchen telephone and orders a large cheese pizza for delivery from Little
Nero's. A short while later the delivery driver pulls up and Kevin pays for
the pizza through the mail slot, scaring the driver off with gangster-movie
gunfire played from a VCR tape.
@expect requires mccallister_phone | status | working

## SCENE 10
INT. MCCALLISTER HOUSE - NIGHT. Kevin overhears Harry and Marv outside
planning to break into his house at nine o'clock. He decides to defend his
home, sketches a battle plan, and rigs the house with traps — a blowtorch over
the back door, a hot doorknob, tar on the basement stairs, paint cans on the
staircase.
@expect requires kevin | location | mccallister_house
@expect establishes kevin | knowledge | bandits_plan
@expect establishes mccallister_house | status | trapped

## SCENE 11
INT./EXT. MCCALLISTER HOUSE - NIGHT. Harry and Marv break in at nine and walk
into trap after trap. Battered and furious, they chase Kevin out of the house.
Kevin runs across the street to the flooded Murphy house, where the bandits
finally corner him.
@expect requires mccallister_house | status | trapped
@expect requires wet_bandits | plan | rob_mccallister_house

## SCENE 12
EXT. MURPHY HOUSE - NIGHT. Old Man Marley appears out of the dark and drops
the bandits with his snow shovel, carrying Kevin home. Police arrive moments
later and arrest Harry and Marv.
@expect establishes wet_bandits | status | arrested

## SCENE 13
INT. MCCALLISTER HOUSE - CHRISTMAS MORNING. Kate finally makes it home in the
back of a polka band's box truck. The rest of the family arrives minutes
later, straight from the airport. Kevin greets them; he never tells anyone
what happened.
@expect establishes kate | location | mccallister_house
@expect establishes mccallister_family | location | mccallister_house
