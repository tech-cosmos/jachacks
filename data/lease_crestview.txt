OFFICE LEASE AGREEMENT

This Office Lease Agreement (the "Lease") is entered into as of March 1,
2026, by and between CRESTVIEW COMMONS LP, a Delaware limited partnership
("Landlord"), and NORTHWIND ROBOTICS, INC., a California corporation
("Tenant").

SECTION 1 — PREMISES AND TERM. Landlord leases to Tenant Suite 400 of the
building located at 2200 Crestview Parkway, San Jose, California (the
"Premises"), comprising approximately 6,200 rentable square feet. The term
of this Lease is sixty (60) months, commencing April 1, 2026.

SECTION 2 — BASE RENT. Tenant shall pay base rent of eight thousand four
hundred dollars ($8,400) per month, due on the first day of each calendar
month.

SECTION 3 — PARKING. Landlord grants Tenant a parking license for ten (10)
reserved parking spaces in Garage B, marked with Tenant's name, at no
additional charge for the duration of the term.

SECTION 4 — SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of
twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000) as a security deposit, to be held for
the duration of the term.

SECTION 5 — SIGNAGE. Tenant is granted the exclusive right to install and
maintain one illuminated rooftop sign bearing Tenant's logo, subject to
municipal approval.

SECTION 6 — SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not sublet the Premises or any part
thereof without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be
unreasonably withheld.
