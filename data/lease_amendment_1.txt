FIRST AMENDMENT TO OFFICE LEASE AGREEMENT

This First Amendment (the "Amendment") to the Office Lease Agreement dated
March 1, 2026 between CRESTVIEW COMMONS LP ("Landlord") and NORTHWIND
ROBOTICS, INC. ("Tenant") is entered into as of September 15, 2026.

SECTION 1 — RENT REDUCTION. In consideration of the concessions set forth
below, base rent is reduced to seven thousand nine hundred dollars ($7,900)
per month, effective October 1, 2026.

SECTION 2 — SURRENDER OF PARKING. Tenant's parking license under Section 3
of the Lease is TERMINATED in its entirety, and all of Tenant's reserved
spaces in Garage B are surrendered to Landlord. Effective October 1, 2026,
Tenant shall hold zero (0) reserved parking spaces, and Tenant shall remove
its name markings from the surrendered spaces by that date.

SECTION 3 — VISITOR PARKING. Tenant's visitors shall be entitled to park,
at no charge, in Tenant's ten (10) reserved spaces in Garage B pursuant to
Tenant's parking license under Section 3 of the Lease, and Tenant shall
install directional signage within Garage B identifying those reserved
spaces for its visitors.

SECTION 4 — RATIFICATION. Except as expressly amended herein, all terms and
conditions of the Lease remain in full force and effect.
