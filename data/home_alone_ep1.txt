# TITLE: Home Alone (1990) — Part 1
# CANON: home-alone
# KIND: film script
# VOCAB: kevin, kate, mccallister_family, mccallister_phone, mccallister_house, alarm_clocks, house_power, wet_bandits, murphy_house

## SCENE 1
INT. MCCALLISTER HOUSE - NIGHT. The house is chaos on the eve of the family's
Christmas trip to Paris. Eight-year-old Kevin fights with his older brother
Buzz over the last cheese pizza and is sent by his mother to sleep upstairs in
the attic bedroom as punishment. Kevin tells her he wishes his family would
disappear.
@expect establishes kevin | location | attic
@expect establishes mccallister_family | plan | paris_trip

## SCENE 2
EXT. MCCALLISTER HOUSE - LATER THAT NIGHT. A violent winter storm batters the
neighborhood. A heavy tree branch snaps under the wind and ice and crashes
down across the telephone lines outside, tearing them from the pole. Telephone
service to the McCallister house is knocked out. The power also blinks out for
a moment, silently resetting every alarm clock in the house.
@expect establishes mccallister_phone | status | down
@expect establishes alarm_clocks | status | reset

## SCENE 3
INT. MCCALLISTER HOUSE - MORNING. The family oversleeps — the reset alarm
clocks never rang. In the frantic scramble to make the flight, a neighbor kid
in the garage is miscounted as one of the children, and the airport shuttle
vans tear off without anyone checking the attic. Kevin, still asleep upstairs,
is left behind.
@expect requires alarm_clocks | status | reset
@expect establishes mccallister_family | location | airport
@expect establishes kevin | location | mccallister_house

## SCENE 4
INT. AIRPLANE - DAY. Somewhere over the Atlantic, Kate McCallister bolts
upright in her seat with a gasp: they forgot Kevin.
@expect establishes kate | knowledge | kevin_left_behind

## SCENE 5
INT. PARIS AIRPORT - DAY. Kate rushes to a bank of payphones to call the
house. An operator tells her the storm has downed the telephone lines in
Kevin's neighborhood and her call cannot go through. She cannot reach her son.
@expect requires mccallister_phone | status | down
@expect establishes mccallister_phone | status | down

## SCENE 6
EXT. LINCOLN AVENUE - DAY. Harry and Marv, the self-styled "Wet Bandits,"
cruise the block in a panel van, noting which families have left for the
holidays. They mark the McCallister house as empty and plan to break in.
@expect establishes wet_bandits | plan | rob_mccallister_house

## SCENE 7
INT. GROCERY STORE - DAY. Kevin, on his own, walks to the store and does the
family shopping with cash he found around the house — milk, orange juice,
laundry detergent, and a toothbrush.
@expect requires kevin | possession | cash

## SCENE 8
INT. PARIS AIRPORT - NIGHT. Unable to reach the house on any line, Kate burns
through standby lists at every airline counter, trying to book any seat on any
flight back to Chicago.
@expect requires mccallister_phone | status | down
