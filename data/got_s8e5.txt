# TITLE: Game of Thrones — S8E5 "The Bells"
# KIND: tv episode script
# VOCAB: dothraki_horde, unsullied, jon_snow, daenerys, drogon, kings_landing, golden_company

## SCENE 1
INT. DRAGONSTONE - DAY. Daenerys, grieving and furious, plans the assault on
King's Landing with Grey Worm and Jon: the fleet will hit the Iron Fleet,
Drogon will burn the scorpions, and her ground forces will storm the gates.
@expect establishes daenerys | plan | assault_kings_landing

## SCENE 2
EXT. KING'S LANDING, CITY GATES - DAY. The gates fall. The Dothraki horde,
thousands of screamers strong, charges on horseback through the breach and
into the streets, arakhs flashing, cutting down the fleeing remnants of the
Golden Company alongside the Unsullied advance.
@expect requires dothraki_horde | status | battle_ready

## SCENE 3
EXT. KING'S LANDING - DAY. The bells of surrender ring out across the city.
Daenerys, atop Drogon, hears them — and turns toward the Red Keep, burning
the city and its people street by street.
@expect establishes kings_landing | status | burning
