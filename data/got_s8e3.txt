# TITLE: Game of Thrones — S8E3 "The Long Night"
# KIND: tv episode script
# VOCAB: dothraki_horde, unsullied, jon_snow, daenerys, arya, night_king, winterfell, army_of_the_dead

## SCENE 1
EXT. WINTERFELL - NIGHT. The living assemble in battle lines before the
castle: the full Dothraki horde on horseback at the front, arakhs raised,
with the Unsullied phalanx and Northern infantry behind them. Melisandre
rides the line and sets every Dothraki blade alight with a word.
@expect establishes dothraki_horde | location | winterfell
@expect establishes dothraki_horde | status | battle_ready

## SCENE 2
EXT. FIELDS BEFORE WINTERFELL - NIGHT. The Dothraki charge screaming into
the darkness toward the army of the dead. From the battlements, the distant
flaming arakhs wink out, rank after rank, until the field goes silent and
black. A handful of riderless horses gallop back. The Dothraki horde has
been wiped out in the first minutes of the battle.
@expect establishes dothraki_horde | status | wiped_out

## SCENE 3
INT. WINTERFELL, GODSWOOD - NIGHT. As the Night King reaches for Bran, Arya
leaps from the dark and destroys him with the dagger. Every white walker and
wight shatters where it stands. The battle for the North is over, won at
staggering cost to the armies of the living.
@expect establishes night_king | alive | false
@expect establishes army_of_the_dead | status | destroyed
